Our Story

Built for the Pharma Industry

Founded over a decade ago, Zepfter was created with a singular mission: to help pharmaceutical and life sciences companies harness the full power of technology while staying rigorously compliant.

We saw a gap in the market — generic IT firms applying standard solutions to highly specialized pharma challenges. Zepfter was built to be different: a team of pharma veterans and technology experts who speak both languages fluently.

Today, with 600+ professionals, 350+ implementations, and clients across 15+ countries, Zepfter is one of the most trusted pharma IT partners in the life sciences industry.
