Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Zepfter Pvt. Ltd. ("Zepfter", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard personal information when you visit zepfter.com or contact us.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details: name, email, phone, company name, and message content submitted via our contact form.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: business email address when you opt in to Zepfter Insights.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referral source via standard web server logs and analytics.

How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to inquiries, demo requests, and support requests.
  • Send newsletters you have subscribed to (with unsubscribe option).
  • Improve our website, services, and user experience.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect against fraud.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share information with trusted service providers (email delivery, form processing, hosting) under contractual confidentiality obligations, or when required by law.

Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained for up to 3 years for business correspondence. Newsletter data is retained until you unsubscribe.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal data. Contact us at info@zepfter.com.

Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect data. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
