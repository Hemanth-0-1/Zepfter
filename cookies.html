Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Zepfter Pvt. Ltd. uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites function and remember preferences.

Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic site functionality and form security.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand traffic patterns and improve content (if enabled).
  • Preference cookies: Remember settings such as newsletter subscription state in local storage.

Local Storage

We use browser local storage to temporarily save newsletter sign-ups and form submission logs for demo/fallback purposes on this static site.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect site functionality.
